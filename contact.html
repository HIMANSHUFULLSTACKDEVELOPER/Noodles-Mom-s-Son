Got Questions? We've Got Answers!

Whether you want to place a bulk order, ask about a product, or just say hello — we're always happy to hear from you. Use the form, WhatsApp us, or check the FAQs below.

📦 How do I place an order?

The easiest way is to message us on WhatsApp at +91 98810 47800. You can also use the contact form or email us at info@noodlesmomsson.in. We'll confirm your order and arrange delivery!

🚚 What is the delivery time?

We dispatch same day for orders placed before 2 PM. Delivery typically takes 1–3 business days depending on your location in India. Free delivery on orders above ₹499!

🌶️ Are your products preservative-free?

Absolutely! All our products are made with zero preservatives, zero artificial colours, and zero shortcuts. We source directly from farms and prepare everything in small, fresh batches.

🎁 Do you offer bulk or gift orders?

Yes! We offer special bulk pricing and curated gift hampers for festivals, weddings, and corporate gifting. Contact us directly on WhatsApp for custom hamper enquiries.

💳 What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfer, and cash on delivery for local Nagpur orders. All payment details are shared over WhatsApp after order confirmation.

🔄 What is your return policy?

Customer satisfaction is our priority. If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, reach out within 24 hours via WhatsApp with a photo and we'll resolve it immediately — replacement or refund, your choice.
